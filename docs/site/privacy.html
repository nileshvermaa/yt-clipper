Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

gta6-clipper is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What data is accessed

The app requests the youtube.upload permission for the owner's Google account. It uses this only to upload videos and their titles, descriptions and tags to that channel. It does not read, collect or store any other Google user data.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
